Arena Wreck – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Arena Wreck (package name com.arenawreck.game) handles user data. In short: Arena Wreck does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or sensitive data.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the game saves a small amount of game data only on your own device:

  • game settings (for example sound, vibration, difficulty, number of enemies),
  • your chosen car and arena,
  • your best results for each difficulty, and whether you have completed the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The only permission Arena Wreck uses is Vibrate, so it can give haptic feedback during play. Vibration can be turned off in the game's settings.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Arena Wreck contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell, trade or share any data with third parties.

Children

Arena Wreck does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Google Play

When you download the game, Google Play may collect information under Google's own privacy policy. That is handled by Google, not by Arena Wreck.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
